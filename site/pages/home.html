Academic profile

I am an Assistant Professor in Operations Research in the Information and Operations Management Department at ESCP Business School in Madrid. My research focuses on airline competition, aviation emissions and noise, and power grid expansion under uncertainty. I use mathematical optimization and game-theoretic models to assess market outcomes and public policy.
